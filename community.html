Ground Rules

Community Rules

To keep Genesis Tech Lab a positive and productive environment, all members are expected to follow these guidelines.

Be Respectful Treat every member with respect. No bullying, harassment, or discrimination of any kind. Everyone is here to learn.
Stay On Topic Keep discussions relevant to technology, learning, and the topics covered in sessions. Off-topic content should be minimal.
Ask Freely, Answer Kindly There are no stupid questions. Encourage questions and always answer with patience and clarity.
Respect Privacy Do not share personal information of other members or share content from private sessions outside the community.
No Spam or Promotions Do not share promotional content, advertisements, or irrelevant links without admin approval.
Participate Actively The community thrives when everyone contributes. Share your learning, ask questions, and celebrate others' wins.
